The beta service

Syllabloom organizes course information, makes notes, concepts, and review cards from supported sources, tracks study activity, and exports approved cards to Anki. You decide which generated material to keep or export.

Features may be incomplete, change, or become unavailable during testing. Hosted lecture transcription isn't enabled. Audio and video in the media library stay in the browser on the device where you added them and don't sync across devices.

Source parsing and card generation can miss context or produce errors. Syllabloom is a study aid, not a substitute for course instruction, official requirements, or your judgment.

Your materials

You keep your rights to the course materials and other content you provide. You allow Syllabloom to receive, process, display, sync, transform, and export that content only as needed to run the features you choose and maintain the beta.

Only submit content you have permission to use. You are responsible for following copyright rules, school policies, and any consent requirements before recording or sharing a class, lecturer, or other student. Avoid uploading sensitive personal information that is not needed for study.

To generate cards, Syllabloom sends extracted text from documents categorized as class materials and past assessments to OpenAI's API. The original uploaded file is not sent to OpenAI. Documents categorized as syllabi are excluded from card generation; if you manually override a document's type, the selected type controls processing. The Privacy Policy describes provider handling and retention. You authorize this processing when you choose to upload a supported source for card generation. Do not submit confidential or sensitive information unless you have permission and are comfortable with that processing.

Study materials and Anki exports

AI-generated cards are drafts. Syllabloom checks for a matching source passage, but that doesn't guarantee factual accuracy, completeness, or suitability for a course. Check each card and passage against your source before studying or exporting. Anki is a separate product. After you download and import a package, your Anki setup and the applicable Anki terms govern its storage, scheduling, and use.

Exported decks may not preserve every collection-wide Anki setting. Keep your own copies of important study data and check a package after import.
