# Upload-to-study release

Keep the existing cream, ink and green theme, typography and companion artwork. Green stays the primary action; yellow and pink retain their existing supporting roles. No visual rebrand.

The primary route is sign in, upload a lecture, inspect three real cards, study five. Course naming, syllabus, Anki settings and question style are optional. Returning students continue their latest source or add the next lecture.

Coverage means sections with cited cards, never completeness or mastery. Empty or sparse extracted text stays visible. Selected-section drafting uses the same durable queue and server idempotency as normal imports. Previous cards and deletions must survive.

Measure signed-in activation, study on separate UTC days and verified payments. Founder and research cohorts are separate from organic users. No document content or names in analytics; 90-day event retention and device opt-out. Aggregate counts are not ordered conversion rates. Gross receipts exclude refunds and fees.

Validation: regression suite, live import with a new file, previews, five-card completion, selected-section drafting, report access, narrow and wide layouts. Human content accuracy, actual phones, voluntary return and paid demand require the student research round.
