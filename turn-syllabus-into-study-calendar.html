Bring syllabus dates into a calendar you can check

A syllabus may mix exam dates, weekly topics, reading ranges, assignment windows, and days without class. Syllabloom looks for actual course events instead of treating every number as a deadline. It adds supported dates to the calendar with the source context attached.

If the year is unclear or the text doesn't point to one specific day, Syllabloom holds the item for review instead of choosing a date. If the syllabus has no dated schedule, it leaves the calendar empty rather than inventing an exam.

See the dates that matter next

A short list of upcoming exams, quizzes, assignments, and class meetings is often more useful than a crowded month view. Syllabloom also separates course dates from general holiday references.

Dates added from a syllabus can link back to the file and the text they came from. U.S. federal holidays are only a reference; your school sets its own closure calendar.

Import dates from a schedule photo

If an instructor posts a schedule as a photo or screenshot, Syllabloom can read a clear image on your device. Before saving anything, you can correct the event names and dates, choose an event type, or remove anything it misread.

This works best with sharp images that show individual dates. It won't expand a repeating weekly timetable, and a blurry image may not produce reliable events. The photo is read on your device and is not saved as a hosted document. Enter repeating events yourself or import them from the syllabus.

Use course dates to set study priorities

An upcoming exam can change what Syllabloom puts in your next study session. Previous answers and missed concepts can also affect what appears. The app only knows your instructor's priorities when they're included in the materials you add.

Always use the current course outline and instructor updates as the final authority. If an exam moves, edit the event. If an announcement isn't in your uploaded files, add it yourself. Then check that the next session covers what you actually need to learn.

Questions students ask

Does Syllabloom invent missing exam dates?

No. If the syllabus has no dated schedule, Syllabloom won't invent dates. It asks you to review unclear years and won't treat unsupported date ranges as confirmed events.

Does a federal holiday mean campus is closed?

No. A federal holiday can be shown as a planning reference, but the calendar marks it as unconfirmed because campus schedules vary.

Can I add dates myself?

Yes. The calendar supports manual course events as well as syllabus dates and editable candidates from an imported schedule image.

What gets added

  • A date stated in the course material.
  • A useful course event label.
  • A source or review step when needed.

Before you rely on it

Compare important dates with the current instructor announcement. Syllabus text and screenshots can be outdated or misread.
