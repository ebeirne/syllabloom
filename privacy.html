How Syllabloom handles your data

Here's what happens to your data when you set up a class, add materials, study, or export cards.

The short version Your class and study workspace sync to your account. Original audio and video stay in this browser. If you choose automatic lecture processing, a private temporary copy is uploaded to the AWS worker and deleted after processing.

Who runs Syllabloom

An individual project owner currently operates Syllabloom Beta. No separate company or legal entity has been formed. Send privacy questions or requests to the email below.

What information we handle

  • Account information: Clerk provides sign-in and account identity, such as your account identifier and email address. If you choose Google sign-in, Google also handles that authentication.
  • Class and study workspace: class names and settings, course dates, calendar entries, source names and derived notes or concepts, draft and approved cards, review history, study progress, and Anki preferences. When signed in, this workspace is stored in Neon Postgres and associated with your account.
  • Supported course documents: when you submit a DOCX, PowerPoint (including older PPT files), PDF, or TXT source, the beta extracts readable text to prepare a preview and study material. PowerPoint speaker notes and image descriptions are included when present; slide pixels are not sent to the AI service. Large uploads pass through private, short-lived storage on our AWS server so the app can receive them without making the document public. The app deletes the temporary copy after processing. A daily cleanup removes abandoned uploads after 24 hours. For non-syllabus sources, extracted text is sent in bounded batches to Syllabloom's API and then to OpenAI to draft source-grounded cards; the original file is not sent to OpenAI. Unfinished imports, including the original file and extracted text, are saved on this device for up to 24 hours to support recovery as described below. Syllabloom syncs the resulting workspace data, including cards and supporting source excerpts, not a cloud library of the original document.
  • AI card-generation data: Syllabloom sends extracted text from class materials and past assessments to OpenAI's Responses API and receives draft cards with source excerpts. Syllabloom requests non-persisted response state with store: false. OpenAI's standard API data controls currently say Responses API content is not used to train models and that abuse-monitoring logs may retain prompts and responses for up to 30 days; store: false does not turn off that separate abuse-monitoring retention. Daily character totals and a hashed account identifier are retained in the app database for up to 90 days to enforce usage limits. Aggregate monthly budget reservations contain no account identifier. Documents categorized as syllabi are parsed locally for calendar dates and are not sent through this card-generation flow; if you manually override a document's type, the selected type controls processing.
  • Source coverage: readable source sections, their page or slide labels and draft cards are saved with your workspace so you can inspect citations and generate more cards from selected sections. Remove the source to remove it from your active workspace.
  • Product usage: signed-in usage events record steps such as imports and completed study sessions, a hashed account identifier, session identifier, counts, and optional referral codes. They do not contain filenames, course text or answers. Events are retained for 90 days. Turn off usage sharing for this browser in Profile. Verified payment events are recorded separately from this optional browser tracking.
  • On-device OCR: if a PDF contains scanned pages, the browser can render up to 100 pages and read them on your device using PDF.js and the Tesseract.js OCR library with its English-language model. The source PDF may still be temporarily uploaded for document preflight as described above; the rendered page images are not sent to OpenAI or the OCR CDN. OCR text is handled like other extracted course text and is sent for card generation only for class materials or past assessments. For the calendar photo importer, OCR runs in your browser; the schedule photo and full extracted text stay on your device and are discarded after review. If you confirm a detected date, up to 500 characters from its matching OCR line are saved with the calendar event so you can check why it was added; signed-in events sync with your workspace. Unconfirmed candidates are not saved.
  • Audio and video: original recordings and uploaded media stay in browser storage on the device where you add them and aren't included in cross-device cloud sync. When automatic lecture processing is available and you use it, the app uploads a private temporary copy to the AWS processing worker. The worker extracts audio, creates a timestamped transcript, and may select compact video frames when the scene changes. It deletes the temporary media after the job finishes. Interrupted uploads are removed by scheduled cleanup after 24 hours. Processing results, including transcript text and selected frame thumbnails, can remain in private job storage for up to seven days so the browser can recover a completed job.
  • Anki exports: when you request an export, approved card content and selected settings are sent to the export service to create an Anki package. Syllabloom does not provide a cloud archive of exported packages.
  • Technical information: the hosting, authentication, and database services may process ordinary request, device, security, and diagnostic information needed to operate and protect the beta.

How information is used

We use this information to sign you in, save and sync your class workspace, prepare study materials, track your progress, make the exports you request, provide support, and keep the service secure.

The beta uses an AI service to draft cards from extracted course text. Only text from documents categorized as class materials or past assessments is sent to OpenAI, not the original uploaded file. Syllabi are parsed locally for calendar dates unless you change the document type. Syllabloom checks each accepted draft for a matching source passage, but that does not guarantee it is complete or correct. Check cards and passages against your course materials before studying or exporting.

Service providers

Syllabloom uses Clerk for authentication, Neon for signed-in workspace storage and AI usage limits, Amazon Web Services (AWS) to host this deployment and temporarily process uploads, and OpenAI to generate cards from extracted course text. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and under their own terms and privacy notices. When you use OCR, the browser loads PDF.js or the Tesseract.js library, worker, OCR engine, and English-language model on demand from jsDelivr. That CDN can receive ordinary connection metadata, but your PDF page images, schedule photos, and recognized text are not sent to the CDN. Google processes information when you choose its sign-in option.

Paid subscriptions use Stripe-hosted checkout and billing management for Beirne Studios. Payment details are entered directly into Stripe; Syllabloom does not store your full card number. We store your account identifier, lifetime-access grant if applicable, Stripe customer identifier, checkout reference, and subscription verification status to provide access and support. Stripe receives an account reference and the billing information you enter at checkout. Founder/test accounts do not consume the 10 new-member lifetime grants.

Storage and retention

Unfinished document imports are saved in this browser, separately for each signed-in account, so you can resume after a reload. This local recovery copy includes the original file, extracted text and completed drafts. It is removed when the import finishes or you remove it from the queue. Recovery expires after 24 hours; expired copies are cleared when import storage is next opened. Clearing this site's browser data also removes them.

Completed card-generation batches are cached in the app database for recovery for 24 hours, so a lost response can be recovered without another model call. Expired cached drafts are removed on the next import or daily cleanup. Hashed account and request identifiers, processing status and failure markers are retained for up to seven days before cleanup to prevent accidental retries. These checkpoints do not send additional data to OpenAI or change the store: false setting. If a result cannot be recovered, restarting an unfinished batch explicitly requests generation again; completed batches are reused while available.

Your signed-in workspace remains stored so the app can provide class and study features. Original supported documents and lecture processing copies are temporary and deleted after their job finishes. If processing is interrupted, scheduled cleanup removes abandoned temporary copies after 24 hours. Private lecture job results expire after seven days. Extracted document text sent to OpenAI is subject to the API retention described above. Daily AI usage totals associated with a hashed account identifier are deleted after 90 days; aggregate monthly budget reservations do not include account identifiers. Original audio and video remain in browser storage on the device where you added them and do not become a cross-device cloud media library. Hosting providers may retain limited technical logs under their own systems and policies.

To request access to or deletion of your account workspace, email the project owner. We may ask for enough information to verify that the request relates to your account. Some limited records may need to remain for security, legal obligations, or backup rotation.

Your choices

  • You can choose what class materials to add. Do not upload information you do not have permission to use.
  • You can remove local recordings from the device library in the app. Browser settings can also clear local site storage, but that may permanently remove media saved only on that device.
  • You can contact us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of account information. We will review each request and respond, subject to applicable law and technical limits.
  • Depending on where you live, local privacy laws may give you additional rights. Contact us and identify your region so we can assess your request.

Children

Syllabloom is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided account information, contact us so we can review it. People under 18 should use the service only with parent or guardian involvement where required by local law.
