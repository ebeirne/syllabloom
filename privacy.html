Your class stays yours.

This notice explains what Syllabloom handles when you set up a class, add course materials, study, or export cards.

Who runs Syllabloom

Syllabloom Beta is currently operated by an individual project owner. No separate company or legal entity has been formed. Send privacy questions and requests to the contact email below.

Information handled

  • Account information: Clerk provides sign-in and account identity, such as your account identifier and email address. If you choose Google sign-in, Google also handles that authentication.
  • Class and study workspace: class names and settings, course dates, calendar entries, source names and derived notes or concepts, draft and approved cards, review history, study progress, and Anki preferences. When signed in, this workspace is stored in Neon Postgres and associated with your account.
  • Supported course documents: when you submit a DOCX, PPTX, PDF, or TXT source, the beta temporarily processes the file to create a preview and study material. Large uploads pass through private, short-lived Vercel Blob storage so the app can receive them without exposing the document publicly. The app deletes the temporary copy after processing; the browser also attempts cleanup if processing is interrupted, and a daily cleanup removes abandoned uploads after 24 hours. For non-syllabus sources, extracted text is sent to OpenAI's API to draft source-grounded cards; the original document file is not sent to OpenAI. The app syncs the resulting workspace data, including cards and their supporting source excerpts, not a cloud library of the original document.
  • AI card-generation data: Syllabloom sends extracted text from class materials and past assessments to OpenAI's Responses API and receives draft cards with source excerpts. Syllabloom requests non-persisted response state with store: false. OpenAI's standard API data controls currently say Responses API content is not used to train models and that abuse-monitoring logs may retain prompts and responses for up to 30 days; store: false does not turn off that separate abuse-monitoring retention. Daily character totals and a hashed account identifier are retained in the app database for up to 90 days to enforce usage limits. Documents categorized as syllabi are parsed locally for calendar dates and are not sent through this card-generation flow; if you manually override a document's type, the selected type controls processing.
  • Schedule photos: if you choose the calendar photo importer, the browser reads the image on your device using English-language OCR. The image and extracted text are not uploaded or saved by Syllabloom. Only dates and event details you confirm are added to your calendar and synced with your signed-in workspace.
  • Audio and video: recordings and uploaded media are kept in browser storage on the device where you add them. They are not part of the cross-device cloud sync. Clearing browser data or changing devices can make those local files unavailable. Hosted transcription is currently disabled.
  • Anki exports: when you request an export, approved card content and selected settings are sent to the export service to create an Anki package. Syllabloom does not provide a cloud archive of exported packages.
  • Technical information: the hosting, authentication, and database services may process ordinary request, device, security, and diagnostic information needed to operate and protect the beta.

How information is used

Information is used to sign you in, save and sync your class workspace, prepare previews and study materials, record your study progress, create requested exports, provide support, and maintain service security.

The beta uses an AI service to draft cards from extracted course text. Source files are parsed by Syllabloom; only extracted text from documents categorized as class materials or past assessments is sent to OpenAI, not the uploaded file itself. Documents categorized as syllabi are parsed locally for calendar dates and are not sent through this AI card-generation flow; if you manually override a document's type, the selected type controls processing. Syllabloom checks that each accepted draft includes an exact passage from the source, but that does not guarantee the card is complete or correct. Review cards and their cited passage against your original course materials before studying or exporting.

Service providers

Syllabloom uses Clerk for authentication, Neon for signed-in workspace storage and AI usage limits, Vercel to host the app and temporarily process uploads, and OpenAI to generate cards from extracted course text. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and under their own terms and privacy notices. When you start photo import, the browser also loads the Tesseract.js OCR library, worker, OCR engine, and English-language model on demand from jsDelivr. That CDN can receive ordinary connection metadata, but the schedule image and recognized text stay in your browser and are not sent to it by the importer. Google processes information when you choose its sign-in option.

Payments are not enabled in this public beta, and Syllabloom is not currently asking you for payment card details. If that changes, this notice will be updated before paid checkout is offered.

Storage and retention

Your signed-in workspace remains stored so the app can provide your class and study features. Original supported documents are processed temporarily and deleted after processing; if processing is interrupted, the scheduled cleanup removes abandoned temporary copies after 24 hours. Extracted text sent to OpenAI is subject to the API retention described above. Daily AI usage totals associated with a hashed account identifier are deleted after 90 days. Audio and video remain in the browser storage on the device where they were added. Hosting providers may retain limited technical logs under their own systems and policies.

To request access to or deletion of your account workspace, email the project owner. We may ask for enough information to verify that the request relates to your account. Some limited records may need to remain for security, legal obligations, or backup rotation.

Your choices

  • You can choose what class materials to add. Do not upload information you do not have permission to use.
  • You can remove local recordings from the device library in the app. Browser settings can also clear local site storage, but that may permanently remove media saved only on that device.
  • You can contact us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of account information. We will review each request and respond, subject to applicable law and technical limits.
  • Depending on where you live, local privacy laws may give you additional rights. Contact us and identify your region so we can assess your request.

Children

Syllabloom is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided account information, contact us so we can review it. People under 18 should use the service only with parent or guardian involvement where required by local law.
