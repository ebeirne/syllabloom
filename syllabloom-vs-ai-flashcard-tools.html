If AI can make cards, what is Syllabloom for?

A chat tool can draft cards from your notes. Syllabloom also keeps your course files, source passages, edits, and study history together. When you're ready, you can export the cards you want to Anki.

A draft deck still needs work

Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini Notebook, Quizlet, and other tools can turn slides into draft questions. That's useful. You still need to check the answers against your course and decide which cards are worth studying.

Syllabloom keeps the source with each card. It also keeps your syllabus dates, edits, and study history in the same class workspace, so you can find what to review before your next assessment.

Compare what each tool is for

This is not a benchmark of card quality. Whichever tool you use, check its cards against your course material.

A chat assistant
Useful for a focused request or for rewriting a difficult idea. You are responsible for keeping the source, syllabus dates, edits, study history, and export settings organized.
Anki
A mature spaced-repetition app for creating cards and scheduling reviews. Syllabloom prepares an editable set for one class and exports it to Anki; it doesn't replace Anki's scheduler.
Syllabloom
A one-class workspace for lecture materials, source-linked cards, in-app study, and an Anki export you can inspect and edit first.

Keep the source next to the question

A card can sound plausible and still be wrong for your course. A source label takes you back to the slide, document, or note it came from. You can check whether the prompt tests the right idea, whether the answer adds unsupported details, and whether the card is worth keeping.

A source link doesn't prove that a card is accurate. You should review, correct, or remove cards before studying them. The link simply makes that checking easier.

What you can do in Syllabloom

  1. Add lecture files and check each draft card against its source passage.
  2. Add syllabus dates to your calendar and plan study sessions around upcoming assessments.
  3. Edit or remove cards, study in the app, or export the cards you want to Anki.
  4. Review unclear dates yourself. Recording transcription isn't available in this beta.

Try it with a class you're taking now. You'll be able to see whether keeping the sources and cards together saves you time.

Before you study a deck

  • Check that each answer matches the source.
  • Rewrite cards that are too broad or unclear.
  • Remove cards that don't help you study.
  • Check your syllabus dates against the original file.

What still needs your review

Cards and dates can be wrong. Check them against your course materials before you rely on them. Syllabloom can't promise better grades or a deck that needs no editing.
